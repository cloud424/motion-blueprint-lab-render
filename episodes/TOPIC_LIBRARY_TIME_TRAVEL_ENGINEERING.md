# Time-Travel Engineering — original science documentary topic library

**Series position:** A rotating topic family within Motion Blueprint Lab, not a channel rename or separate account. Maintain the existing 3-minute English engineering format, EP numbers, real evidence, 6+ differentiated moving diagrams, and licensing and publication gates. Dramatized time-travel opening is a fictional narrative device; the historical manufacturing process must be evidenced and period-appropriate.

## Example mini-story: Could You Make Salt 1,000 Years Ago?

- Opening: imagine being in a coastal settlement with no modern salt refinery.
- Establish geography, climate, era and actual known process.
- Follow sea water to shallow salt pans, evaporation and rising salinity.
- Explain saturation and onset of crystal growth using animated diagrams.
- Show crystal harvesting, washing and drying only where sourced.
- Compare solar and boiling-brine systems including fuel costs and environmental constraints.
- End on why salt mattered to food preservation and trade.
- Never imply all ancient cultures had identical saltworks.

## Editorial controls

1. Topic must be researched with credible history/science references or authentic museum demonstrations.
2. Use real licensed photos/footage and separately labeled original engineering simulations.
3. Produce six or more mechanically meaningful animated viewpoints; vary camera/information around every 10–15 seconds. No empty R1-style moving level alone.
4. Keep English for global audiences, approximately three minutes and portrait 720x1280.
5. This list is exploratory. First finish the pending EP02 before choosing an EP03 topic.
6. Mix topics with the modern engineering and physics subjects; avoid exclusively serializing a repetitive "time travel" premise.
