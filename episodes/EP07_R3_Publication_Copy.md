## Main description

EP07 | How People Made Sugar Before Modern Factories

Before modern refineries, how did skilled makers turn freshly cut sugarcane into a concentrated, solid sweetener?

Follow the real actions behind the science: harvest, crush, handle juice, boil away water, stir thickening syrup, and understand what happens as jaggery sets. Nine different modern authentic source films illustrate the process alongside three original moving engineering cutaways.

The historical account is about southeast India in the 1790s. All film footage is modern; it is NOT eighteenth-century archival footage. Modern brown table sugar shown near the end is deliberately contrasted with, not presented as, traditional jaggery.

Original English narration, science diagrams and mobile-safe subtitles by Motion Blueprint Lab.
